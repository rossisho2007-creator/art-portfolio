About

I've been drawing seriously since secondary school and have spent the last few years moving from standalone illustration into production art — work that has to function inside a system, not just look good in isolation.

That shift is what most of this portfolio documents. A character sheet has to survive being redrawn forty times. A dialogue sprite has to key cleanly off a base pose so the set stays consistent and cheap to extend. A HUD portrait has to still read once it's cropped into a corner of the screen at a fraction of its size. A keychain design has to hold up as a physical cut acrylic object with a hole punched through it.

I'm studying Information & Technology, which means I'm comfortable on both sides of the handoff — I can talk to engineers about asset pipelines and export constraints without needing a translator.
